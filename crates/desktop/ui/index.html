qurb

Your files stay on the devices you already own, encrypted, and sync directly between them. Nothing is kept on anybody else's server.

Which folder?

Everything in it syncs to your other devices. It can be empty, or it can be a folder you already use.

Write these down

These 24 words are not a backup of your key. They are your key, in a form you can hold.

    Nobody else has a copy — not us, not a server. If you lose these words and lose this device, your files cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. That is not a policy we could choose to relax.
