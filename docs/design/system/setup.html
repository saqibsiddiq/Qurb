Setting up, on both apps

As the four stories people actually go through, each step on the screen it happens on. Since decision 0082 a person's computer is their server, so the usual order is the computer first and the phone scanning it. The 24 words are never asked for unless a phone is lost.

1 · A new person

The computer first, as everyone's own server; then the phone scans it. Six screens, two decisions.

2 · A phone set up again

Reinstalled, or a new phone restored from a Google backup: the key is found, and the computer recognises it. No code.

3 · Removed, and added again

The phone's files stay on the computer, locked, and are its again when the same phone comes back.

4 · A second computer

The phone gives the new computer its key. The 24 words are a link, for a lost phone.
