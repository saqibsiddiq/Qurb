QurbSpace foundation

The design system both apps are built from. Every colour, size and corner here is a token, written once and copied exactly: px on the desktop, dp and sp on the phone, one to one. Pages are drawn only from these parts, so what you approve here is what ships.

1 · Colour

One colour does the talking

The deep green marks what to press and what is yours; everything else is warm neutrals. Blue only means "online", amber only "waiting on you", red only "can't be undone".

How this is used. You mark what you'd change here: a colour, a size, a corner, a component. When the foundation is right, I draw each page from these parts only, phone and desktop, at real size. When a page is approved, it's built by copying these numbers into colors.xml and app.css, and the built screen is compared against the mockup side by side before it's called done.
